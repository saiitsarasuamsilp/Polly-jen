Our story

Beer for the beach life

Polly Jen started as a home-brew rig on a Rawai balcony and a stubborn belief that Phuket deserved a proper local ale — not another imported lager. We kept brewing until the batches got bigger than the balcony.

Now we run a small taproom just off the beach road, brewing in-house and pouring it straight from the tank. Same recipes, same crew, just more stools.
