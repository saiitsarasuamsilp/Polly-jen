Our story

Built around a single fire

"We didn't want a menu you could get anywhere. We wanted a fire you could feel."

Polly Jen started as a wood oven in a rented lot and a promise to two dozen regulars: cook honestly, change with the seasons, and never rush the fire. Eight years later the oven is the same one, the regulars have multiplied, and the promise hasn't moved.

Everything on the menu passes through that fire — bread, vegetables, the coffee beans we roast ourselves each Tuesday. What we can't grow nearby, we don't serve.
